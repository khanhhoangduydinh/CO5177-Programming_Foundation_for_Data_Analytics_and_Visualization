PROBLEM

Predict one scene label from one RGB image: buildings, forest, glacier, mountain, sea, or street.

ASSIGNMENT FIT

17,034 labelled images, six classes and 150 × 150 resolution satisfy the Image Data constraints without using a toy dataset.

FAIR EVALUATION

Only the source training folder is split into train and validation data. The separate source test folder is not used for model selection.

02 — SCENE EXAMPLES & IMAGE QUALITY

WHAT THE MODEL MUST DISTINGUISH

Natural cues: trees, snow, water, roads and building structures can indicate a scene label.

Similar views: mountain and glacier can share rock, sky and snow; street and buildings can share man-made structures.

Fixed size: every image is 150 × 150 pixels, so no padding is needed before the pretrained resize step.

Out of scope: the unlabelled prediction folder is not included in training or evaluation.

05 — FINAL TEST RESULTS & ERROR REVIEW

CONCLUSION

Dataset: Intel Image Classification is large enough for the course, has a suitable 150-pixel resolution, and is easier to explain than a fine-grained astronomy task.

Method: pretrained MobileNetV3 avoids training a deep network from zero while still showing a complete image-analysis pipeline.

Limit: one scene may contain cues from several labels, so a strong score does not mean every real-world photograph can be classified perfectly.

Evidence: the notebook, figures, summary JSON and prediction CSV are generated together from one Run all execution.
